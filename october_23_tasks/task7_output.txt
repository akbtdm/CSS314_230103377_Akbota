TASK 7 - Arithmetic Intensity vs. Memory Bottlenecks
Machine: arm64 Darwin, 10 cores (Apple M4: 4P + 6E), N = 30,000,000 float64 (240 MB per array), median of 5 runs

Kernel A: copy (memory-bound)
Cores |  Time (ms) |  Speedup | Efficiency | Effective GB/s
------------------------------------------------------------
    1 |       6.70 |    1.00x |    100.0% |           71.6
    2 |       5.57 |    1.20x |     60.1% |           86.1
   10 |       5.54 |    1.21x |     12.1% |           86.6

Kernel B: sin*cos+tan (compute-bound)
Cores |  Time (ms) |  Speedup | Efficiency | Effective GB/s
------------------------------------------------------------
    1 |     195.11 |    1.00x |    100.0% |            2.5
    2 |      99.03 |    1.97x |     98.5% |            4.8
   10 |      35.77 |    5.45x |     54.5% |           13.4

Verdict
  Kernel A: copy (memory-bound): efficiency at 10 cores = 12.1% -> STALLED - memory channel saturated
  Kernel B: sin*cos+tan (compute-bound): efficiency at 10 cores = 54.5% -> scales with cores
  Kernel A stalls because it does no arithmetic: every element is 16 bytes of DRAM traffic, and the copy tops out at about 87 GB/s, the shared memory bus limit. More cores only add more requests to the same bus. Kernel B does enough math per byte that the bus is never the limit.
