### Cache-line boundary identification and forensic explanation

**Where does contention drop?** With ten writers on adjacent 8-byte words the sweep takes 346.86 ms (46.1 Mops/s). Separating the slots by 16 B gives 333.34 ms (−3.9 %), 32 B gives 319.86 ms (−4.0 %), 64 B gives 314.28 ms (−1.7 %), and from there the curve is flat: 128 B (316.48 ms) and 256 B (311.69 ms) are within the 5–17 ms IQR of the 64 B point. The sharp part of the drop is therefore between **8 and 32 bytes** of separation (−7.8 % cumulatively), the plateau is reached at **64 bytes**, and there is no additional step at the 128-byte line that `sysctl` reports (and that the audit expects for Apple Silicon). Either the store path resolves ownership at a 64-byte granularity, or the residual two-writers-per-line effect at 64 B is smaller than this measurement can resolve. The whole false-sharing penalty is 10.4 % (346.86 vs 314.28 ms), not an order of magnitude, because at p = 1 one read-modify-write step costs 94.8 ns of interpreter work (1516.65 ms / 1.6 × 10⁷) and issues only one 8-byte store; a coherence miss of tens of nanoseconds every ~95 ns is largely absorbed by the store buffer.

**Karp-Flatt trajectories.** e(p) for stride 1 vs stride 16: p = 2: 0.0477 vs 0.0546; p = 4: 0.0481 vs 0.0447; p = 8: 0.1278 vs 0.1097; p = 10: 0.1368 vs 0.1189. Up to four workers the two layouts are indistinguishable (the p = 2 difference even has the "wrong" sign, i.e. it is noise). Both curves then jump between p = 4 and p = 8 — e(p) more than doubles even with isolated cache lines — because workers 5–10 run on efficiency cores and the equal M/p split makes the slowest worker the critical path, which Karp-Flatt reports as serial fraction; the fixed ≈80 ms spawn/join cost (≈25 % of a 315 ms trial at p = 10) adds to it. On top of that, stride 1 is 7.0 % slower than stride 16 at p = 8 (359.17 vs 335.62 ms) and 7.6 % slower at p = 10 (338.46 vs 314.43 ms), which shows up as a further +0.018 in e(p). So e(p) rises in both cases because of core heterogeneity and spawn overhead, and rises faster under stride 1 because the coherence-contention term grows with the number of writers sharing the line. Speedup saturates at 4.8 on ten cores for the isolated layout (E₁₀ = 0.48) and 4.5 for the shared one.

### Amdahl ceiling and core-heterogeneity inflection analysis

**Amdahl cap vs observation.** At p = 1 the serial Stage A is s = 0.0139 of the 699.27 ms trial, so S∞ = 1/s = 72.1 and Amdahl predicts S(10) = 8.89. Observed S_strong(10) = 4.90, i.e. 55 % of the prediction; efficiency falls from 0.97 (p = 2) to 0.85 (p = 4) and 0.49 (p = 10). Amdahl is not violated, it is just not the binding limit: its only hardware assumption, "p identical workers", does not hold on an M4.

**Inflection at p = P_phys → P_phys + 1.** There is no SMT boundary (P_phys = P_log = 10); the architectural boundary is 4 performance → efficiency cores. Marginal speedup ΔS = S_p − S_{p−1}: 0.939, 0.762, 0.698 for workers 2–4, then 0.256, 0.293, 0.230, 0.243, 0.211, 0.269 for workers 5–10. Across p = 4 → 5 the marginal speedup drops from 0.698 to 0.256, a **−63 %** change for one added worker; averaged per regime, workers 2–4 add 0.80 of a core each and workers 5–10 add 0.25 each (−69 %). Going from 4 to 10 workers only shortens the trial from 205.79 ms to 142.71 ms (×1.44), so the six efficiency cores together are worth ≈1.8 performance-core equivalents (≈0.29 each) for this kernel, including the clock reduction the P-cores take when all ten cores are loaded. The per-worker rates in Phase 3A (all ten within 0.127–0.152 GFLOP/s, no fast/slow split) show that macOS time-slices processes across both clusters rather than leaving a single straggler, but the aggregate capacity still grows only by ≈0.25 core-equivalents per added worker beyond four.

**Weak scaling.** With constant work per worker the trial still grows from 117.40 ms (p = 1) to 239.89 ms (p = 10), so E_weak(10) = 0.489. The serial Stage A grows linearly with N(p) (α from 0.0138 to 0.0692) and the parallel Stage B itself slows down as workers land on efficiency cores. Gustafson's scaled speedup from α alone is S_G(10) = 10 − 0.0692 × 9 = 9.38, almost ideal, while the measured efficiency is 0.49: the formula only knows the serial fraction and assumes the parallel part keeps its per-worker time at any p, which is false on a heterogeneous, power-limited laptop CPU.

### Roofline and cache-capacity analysis

**Ceilings.** Ten workers each copying a 6.4 MB slice between two 64 MB shared buffers: median worker copy 1.193 ms, so the lab formula gives **BW_sustained = 2 × 64 × 10⁶ / T_median = 107.26 GB/s**. The per-worker times are bimodal — four workers at ≈0.6 ms (≈21 GB/s of read+write traffic each, the performance cores) and six at ≈1.2–1.3 ms (≈10 GB/s each, the efficiency cores) — so the copies do not fully overlap and the median is an efficiency-core time. Dividing the traffic actually moved (9 reps × 10 workers × 2 × 6.4 MB = 1.15 GB) by the wall time of the whole block (13.1 ms) gives **87.9 GB/s** (87–94 GB/s over three rounds), which is the physically meaningful sustained DRAM bandwidth (the M4's LPDDR5X interface peaks at ≈120 GB/s); both numbers are reported. The compute ceiling is **P_sustained = 1.2724 GFLOP/s** for ten barrier-synchronised 16-way-unrolled FMA loops (6 × 10⁷ FLOPs each; per worker 0.127–0.152 GFLOP/s, i.e. ≈15 ns per `x = x*a + b` statement — the cost of ~5 bytecodes, not of the FP unit). Ridge point **I\* = 1.2724 / 107.26 = 0.0119 FLOP/Byte** (0.0145 with the wall-clock bandwidth): in pure Python every kernel with more than ≈0.01 FLOP per byte is compute-(interpreter-)bound.

**GEMM and BS\* vs cache sizes.** All three variants agree bit-for-bit (max|C(V1) − C(V2)| = max|C(V3) − C(V2)| = 0, same k-summation order). V1 (i-j-k) 0.2081 GFLOP/s, V2 (i-k-j) 0.4589 GFLOP/s, V3 blocked: 0.2289 (BS 8), 0.2575 (16), 0.2689 (32), 0.2729 (64), 0.2780 (96), 0.2777 (128). Nominal **BS\* = 96**, but BS = 32…128 lie within 3.4 % of each other while the IQRs are 4–9 % of the medians: the curve saturates at BS ≥ 32. Tile footprints W = 3·BS²·8 B: 1.5 KB (8), 6 KB (16), 24.6 KB (32), 98 KB (64), 221 KB (96), 393 KB (128). Capacity reasoning predicts a peak where the tile fills L1d — BS ≈ 48 for the 64 KB efficiency-core L1d, BS ≈ 64 for the 128 KB performance-core L1d — followed by a drop; the measured curve rises up to that region but never drops, and the 221 KB tile of BS\* = 96 fits only in L2 (4 MB / 16 MB), not in L1d. The inner statement `c_local[c_off + j] += a_ik * B[b_off + j]` costs ≈100 ns of interpreter time, which hides an L2 hit (and a prefetched DRAM access) completely; what the sweep measures is **loop overhead**: small tiles pay the `range()` setup, the `a_ik` load and the offset arithmetic once per BS multiply-adds, and growing BS amortises that (+21 % from BS 8 to BS 96) until it no longer matters. With N = 256 the whole B matrix is 512 KB and A + B + C = 1.57 MB sit in L2 for the entire run, so there is no DRAM traffic to tile away in the first place.

**Roofline mapping.** With I\* ≈ 0.012 FLOP/Byte every variant is on the flat compute roof: attainable = min(P_sustained, I × BW) = P_sustained = 1.2724 GFLOP/s both for I_naive ≈ 0.125 FLOP/Byte (V1, V2) and for I_tiled(96) = 96/12 = 8 FLOP/Byte (V3). Achieved: **V1 16.4 %, V2 36.1 %, V3(BS\*) 21.8 %** of attainable. Even the naive kernel's memory limit, 0.125 × 107.26 = 13.4 GFLOP/s, is 10× above the interpreter ceiling. V2 beats the best blocked variant by 1.65× and the naive one by 2.21× because it executes the fewest bytecodes per multiply-add (one index into two local lists, no offset arithmetic, no block bookkeeping); in pure Python the optimisation that works is cutting interpreter work, not cache blocking.

### Chunk-size trade-off analysis

**Static schedules.** Work per item grows as K(i) = 1 + ⌊(i/40)²⌋, so under block-static partitioning worker t (items [800t, 800(t+1))) holds ((t+1)³ − t³)/1000 of the work: 0.1 % for worker 0, 27.1 % for worker 9. Measured active times run from 13 ms (worker 0) to 1491 ms (worker 9), λ = 2.466 (the pure arithmetic value is 2.71; it is lower because the last worker finishes alone on an idle performance core at full clock), and T_wall = 1524.9 ms. Cyclic assignment interleaves heavy and light items: worker times 755–852 ms, λ = 1.048, T_wall = 918.9 ms, 1.66× faster with zero synchronisation. Total active time under the balanced schedules is ≈8.0–8.3 s for 1.07 × 10⁸ sin·cos·sqrt iterations (≈75 ns each), i.e. ≈1.0 ms per item on average and ≈3.0 ms for the heaviest item (K = 39 991).

**Dynamic chunk sweep.** C = 1: T_wall 905.1 ms, λ = 1.006, IQR 2.1 ms (8000 chunks); C = 8: 916.1 ms, λ = 1.017 (1000 chunks); C = 64: 925.6 ms, λ = 1.084 (125 chunks); C = 512: 1195.4 ms, λ = 1.628 (16 chunks). **C\* = 1.**

*C = 1 (synchronisation side).* 8000 acquisitions of the `mp.Value` lock plus the bouncing of the counter's cache line between cores cost a few microseconds each — tens of milliseconds spread over ten workers, below the IQR — because every item is ≈1 ms of work, three orders of magnitude more than a lock round-trip. The contention term would only dominate for microsecond-sized items. *C = 512 (tail-imbalance side).* Only 16 chunks exist for 10 workers; the 15th chunk (items 7168–7679) alone holds 16.5 % of all the work and the last one 11.5 %, so whoever claims them finishes ≈0.8 s after the others (worker times 286–1108 ms, λ = 1.628) and T_wall rises by 32 %, worse than cyclic-static. At C = 64 the last chunk is already 2.4 % of the work (≈0.2 s of tail) and λ starts to move (1.084). The optimum is therefore the smallest chunk: coarse-grained items make the queue overhead invisible while any chunk larger than a few dozen items re-introduces the tail skew. T_wall(block static) = 1524.9 ms vs T_wall(dynamic, C\* = 1) = 905.1 ms: dynamic scheduling removes a 68 % penalty. All wall times include ≈80 ms of process spawn/join, identical for every strategy.

```python
from dataclasses import dataclass
import multiprocessing as mp
import time
from typing import Callable


@dataclass
class BenchStats:
    min_ms: float
    median_ms: float
    iqr_ms: float


def fnv1a_hash(s: str) -> int:
    h = 14695981039346656037
    for b in s.encode("utf-8"):
        h = ((h ^ b) * 1099511628211) & 0xFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFF
    return h


def run_benchmark(fn: Callable[[], None], warmup: int = 2, runs: int = 7) -> BenchStats:
    for _ in range(warmup):
        fn()
    samples = []
    for _ in range(runs):
        t0 = time.perf_counter_ns()
        fn()
        samples.append((time.perf_counter_ns() - t0) / 1e6)
    samples.sort()
    med = samples[runs // 2]
    return BenchStats(samples[0], med, samples[(3 * runs) // 4] - samples[runs // 4])


def emit_verify_tag(phase: str, seed: int, m1: float, m2: float) -> str:
    hw = mp.cpu_count()
    sig = fnv1a_hash(f"{phase}|{seed}|{hw}|{m1:.4f}|{m2:.4f}")
    return f"VERIFY_HASH[{phase}]: {sig:016X} (P_log={hw})"
```

```python
def partition(n: int, parts: int):
    """Split range(n) into `parts` contiguous (start, end) ranges, sizes differing by at most 1."""
    base, extra = divmod(n, parts)
    bounds = []
    start = 0
    for t in range(parts):
        end = start + base + (1 if t < extra else 0)
        bounds.append((start, end))
        start = end
    return bounds


def median(values):
    ordered = sorted(values)
    n = len(ordered)
    mid = n // 2
    return ordered[mid] if n % 2 else 0.5 * (ordered[mid - 1] + ordered[mid])

def doubling_up_to(limit: int):
    """[1, 2, 4, ..., limit] — powers of two below limit, then limit itself."""
    values = []
    p = 1
    while p < limit:
        values.append(p)
        p *= 2
    values.append(limit)
    return values
```

```python
"""Phase 0: CPU/cache topology audit and timer noise-floor calibration (task0_cal.py)."""
import multiprocessing as mp
import sys
import time

from harness import run_benchmark, emit_verify_tag
from labutil import (build_parser, resolve_student_id, results_dir, save_json,
                     hardware_info)

CLOCK_READS = 1_000_000


def measure_clock(n_reads: int):
    """Average perf_counter_ns() call latency and the smallest non-zero tick observed."""
    perf = time.perf_counter_ns
    first = prev = perf()
    min_tick = None
    for _ in range(n_reads):
        now = perf()
        delta = now - prev
        if delta > 0 and (min_tick is None or delta < min_tick):
            min_tick = delta
        prev = now
    avg_ns = (prev - first) / n_reads
    return avg_ns, min_tick or 0


def empty_fn() -> None:
    return None


def main() -> None:
    args = build_parser(__doc__).parse_args()
    sid = resolve_student_id(args.id)

        print(f"  {key:20s} {value}")

    print(f"  {'start method':20s} {mp.get_start_method()}")
    print(f"  {'sys.version':20s} {sys.version.split()[0]}")
```

```python
def attach(name: str) -> shared_memory.SharedMemory:

def fs_worker(shm_name: str, slot_idx: int, iters: int) -> None:
    shm = attach(shm_name)
    arr = (ctypes.c_uint64 * SLOTS).from_buffer(shm.buf)
    acc = arr[slot_idx]
    for i in range(iters):
        acc = (acc * 6364136223846793005 + (i | 1)) & 0xFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFF
        arr[slot_idx] = acc  # forces a store to the shared L1 cache line
```

```python
"""Phase 2: strong (Amdahl) vs weak (Gustafson) scaling with a serial Stage A and a
Pool-parallel Stage B (task2_scaling.py)."""
import math
import multiprocessing as mp
import time

from harness import run_benchmark, emit_verify_tag
from labutil import (build_parser, resolve_student_id, results_dir, save_json,
                     hardware_info, partition, median)

N_STRONG = 1_200_000
N0_WEAK = 200_000
SERIAL_FRACTION = 0.05
STAGE_B_ITERS = 30
WARMUP, TIMED = 2, 7


def stage_a(n_serial: int) -> float:
    """Strictly serial prefix reduction: 10 transcendental ops (5 sin + 5 sqrt) per element."""
    sin, sqrt = math.sin, math.sqrt
    acc = 0.0
    for i in range(n_serial):
        x = 0.5 + (i & 1023) * 1e-3
        x = sqrt(sin(x) + 1.5)
        x = sqrt(sin(x) + 1.5)
        x = sqrt(sin(x) + 1.5)
        x = sqrt(sin(x) + 1.5)
        x = sqrt(sin(x) + 1.5)
        acc += x
    return acc


def stage_b_chunk(bounds) -> float:
    """Embarrassingly parallel kernel: 30 multiply-add + sin iterations per element."""
    start, end = bounds
    sin = math.sin
    a, b = 1.0000001, 1e-3
    acc = 0.0
    for i in range(start, end):
        x = 0.5 + (i & 1023) * 1e-3
        for _ in range(STAGE_B_ITERS):
            x = sin(x) * a + b
        acc += x
    return acc


def run_trial(pool, p: int, n: int, record: list) -> None:
    t0 = time.perf_counter_ns()
    serial_sum = stage_a(int(SERIAL_FRACTION * n))
    t1 = time.perf_counter_ns()
    partial = pool.map(stage_b_chunk, partition(n, p), chunksize=1)
    t2 = time.perf_counter_ns()
    record.append(((t1 - t0) / 1e6, (t2 - t1) / 1e6, serial_sum + sum(partial)))


def sweep(label: str, p_values, n_for_p, warmup: int, timed: int):
    rows = []
    for p in p_values:
        n = n_for_p(p)
        record = []
        with mp.Pool(processes=p) as pool:  # created outside the timed region
            stats = run_benchmark(lambda: run_trial(pool, p, n, record),
                                  warmup=warmup, runs=timed)
        timed_records = record[-timed:]
        row = {"p": p, "n": n, "min_ms": stats.min_ms, "median_ms": stats.median_ms,
               "iqr_ms": stats.iqr_ms,
               "stage_a_ms": median(r[0] for r in timed_records),
               "stage_b_ms": median(r[1] for r in timed_records),
               "checksum": timed_records[-1][2]}
        rows.append(row)
        print(f"[{label}] p={p:2d} N={n:>9}: T={row['median_ms']:9.2f} ms "
              f"(iqr {row['iqr_ms']:.2f})  stageA={row['stage_a_ms']:.2f} ms "
              f"stageB={row['stage_b_ms']:.2f} ms  sum={row['checksum']:.6f}")
    return rows


def add_strong_metrics(rows):
    t1 = rows[0]["median_ms"]
    s = rows[0]["stage_a_ms"] / t1
    prev_speedup = None
    for row in rows:
        p = row["p"]
        row["speedup"] = t1 / row["median_ms"]
        row["efficiency"] = row["speedup"] / p
        row["amdahl_speedup"] = 1.0 / (s + (1.0 - s) / p)
        row["marginal_speedup"] = None if prev_speedup is None else row["speedup"] - prev_speedup
        prev_speedup = row["speedup"]
    return {"serial_fraction_s": s, "amdahl_s_inf": 1.0 / s}


def add_weak_metrics(rows):
    t1 = rows[0]["median_ms"]
    for row in rows:
        p = row["p"]
        row["weak_efficiency"] = t1 / row["median_ms"]
        row["alpha"] = row["stage_a_ms"] / row["median_ms"]
        row["gustafson_speedup"] = p - row["alpha"] * (p - 1)


def print_strong(rows, summary):
    print(f"\nTable 2A — strong scaling, N={rows[0]['n']}  "
          f"s={summary['serial_fraction_s']:.4f}  S_inf=1/s={summary['amdahl_s_inf']:.2f}")
    print(f"{'p':>3} {'T_ms':>10} {'iqr':>7} {'S_p':>7} {'E_p':>7} {'Amdahl':>7} {'dS':>7}")
    for r in rows:
        ds = "-" if r["marginal_speedup"] is None else f"{r['marginal_speedup']:.3f}"
        print(f"{r['p']:>3} {r['median_ms']:>10.2f} {r['iqr_ms']:>7.2f} {r['speedup']:>7.3f} "
              f"{r['efficiency']:>7.3f} {r['amdahl_speedup']:>7.3f} {ds:>7}")


def print_weak(rows):
    print("\nTable 2B — weak scaling, N(p) = p * N0")
    print(f"{'p':>3} {'N':>9} {'T_ms':>10} {'iqr':>7} {'E_weak':>7} {'alpha':>7} {'S_G':>7}")
    for r in rows:
        print(f"{r['p']:>3} {r['n']:>9} {r['median_ms']:>10.2f} {r['iqr_ms']:>7.2f} "
              f"{r['weak_efficiency']:>7.3f} {r['alpha']:>7.4f} {r['gustafson_speedup']:>7.3f}")


def main() -> None:
    args = build_parser(__doc__).parse_args()
    sid = resolve_student_id(args.id)
```

```python
"""Phase 3: empirical Roofline ceilings (3A: parallel memcpy bandwidth on 2 x 64 MB shared buffers,
16-way unrolled scalar FMA ceiling) and progressive cache-blocked GEMM with a tile sweep
(3B: naive i-j-k, reordered i-k-j, 6-loop blocked, N = 256) (task3_roofline_gemm.py)."""
import multiprocessing as mp
import random
import time
from array import array
from multiprocessing import shared_memory

from harness import run_benchmark, emit_verify_tag
from labutil import (build_parser, resolve_student_id, results_dir, save_json,
                     hardware_info, partition, median)

BUF_BYTES = 64_000_000
FLOPS_PER_WORKER = 60_000_000
UNROLL = 16
COPY_REPS = 9
ROUNDS = 3
N = 256
TILE_SIZES = [8, 16, 32, 64, 96, 128]
WARMUP, TIMED = 2, 7
SEED = 20261113
DOUBLE = 8

_A = None   # local array('d') copy of A in each pool worker
_B = None   # local array('d') copy of B in each pool worker
_C = None   # SharedMemory handle for C in each pool worker


def attach(name: str) -> shared_memory.SharedMemory:
    try:
        return shared_memory.SharedMemory(name=name, track=False)
    except TypeError:  # Python < 3.13 has no track= argument
        return shared_memory.SharedMemory(name=name)


# ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- Part 3A
def bw_worker(name_a, name_b, start, end, reps, barrier, out_med_ms, out_t0, out_t1, idx) -> None:
    shm_a, shm_b = attach(name_a), attach(name_b)
    buf_a, buf_b = shm_a.buf, shm_b.buf
    buf_a[start:end] = buf_b[start:end]  # warm-up: fault pages in before timing
    barrier.wait()
    samples = []
    t_first = time.perf_counter_ns()
    for _ in range(reps):
        t0 = time.perf_counter_ns()
        buf_a[start:end] = buf_b[start:end]  # native memcpy of this worker's slice
        t1 = time.perf_counter_ns()
        samples.append((t1 - t0) / 1e6)
    out_med_ms[idx] = median(samples)
    out_t0[idx], out_t1[idx] = t_first, t1
    del buf_a, buf_b
    shm_a.close()
    shm_b.close()


def fma_worker(iters, barrier, out_t0, out_t1, out_sum, idx) -> None:
    a, b = 0.9999999, 1e-7
    x0, x1, x2, x3 = 1.0, 1.1, 1.2, 1.3
    x4, x5, x6, x7 = 1.4, 1.5, 1.6, 1.7
    x8, x9, x10, x11 = 1.8, 1.9, 2.0, 2.1
    x12, x13, x14, x15 = 2.2, 2.3, 2.4, 2.5
    barrier.wait()
    t0 = time.perf_counter_ns()
    for _ in range(iters):
        x0 = x0 * a + b
        x1 = x1 * a + b
        x2 = x2 * a + b
        x3 = x3 * a + b
        x4 = x4 * a + b
        x5 = x5 * a + b
        x6 = x6 * a + b
        x7 = x7 * a + b
        x8 = x8 * a + b
        x9 = x9 * a + b
        x10 = x10 * a + b
        x11 = x11 * a + b
        x12 = x12 * a + b
        x13 = x13 * a + b
        x14 = x14 * a + b
        x15 = x15 * a + b
    t1 = time.perf_counter_ns()
    out_t0[idx], out_t1[idx] = t0, t1
    out_sum[idx] = (x0 + x1 + x2 + x3 + x4 + x5 + x6 + x7
                    + x8 + x9 + x10 + x11 + x12 + x13 + x14 + x15)


def measure_bandwidth(p: int, buf_bytes: int, reps: int) -> dict:
    shm_a = shared_memory.SharedMemory(create=True, size=buf_bytes)
    shm_b = shared_memory.SharedMemory(create=True, size=buf_bytes)
    try:
        pattern = bytes(range(256)) * (buf_bytes // 256) + bytes(range(buf_bytes % 256))
        # macOS rounds the mapping up to a 16 KB page, so slice to the requested size
        shm_b.buf[:buf_bytes] = pattern
        shm_a.buf[:buf_bytes] = b"\x00" * buf_bytes
        del pattern
        barrier = mp.Barrier(p)
        out = mp.Array("d", p)
        t0s, t1s = mp.Array("q", p), mp.Array("q", p)
        procs = [mp.Process(target=bw_worker,
                            args=(shm_a.name, shm_b.name, s, e, reps, barrier, out, t0s, t1s, idx))
                 for idx, (s, e) in enumerate(partition(buf_bytes, p))]
        for proc in procs:
            proc.start()
        for proc in procs:
            proc.join()
        copy_ok = (bytes(shm_a.buf[:4096]) == bytes(shm_b.buf[:4096])
                   and bytes(shm_a.buf[buf_bytes - 4096:buf_bytes])
                   == bytes(shm_b.buf[buf_bytes - 4096:buf_bytes]))
        per_worker = list(out)
        t_med_ms = median(per_worker)
        bw_gbs = 2 * buf_bytes / (t_med_ms * 1e-3) / 1e9  # lab formula: assumes full overlap
        wall_ns = max(t1s) - min(t0s)                    # all reps of all workers, first start -> last end
        bw_wall_gbs = 2 * buf_bytes * reps / wall_ns     # bytes per ns == GB/s
        return {"t_median_worker_ms": t_med_ms, "per_worker_ms": per_worker,
                "bw_gbs": bw_gbs, "wall_ms": wall_ns / 1e6, "bw_wallclock_gbs": bw_wall_gbs,
                "copy_verified": copy_ok}
    finally:
        shm_a.close()
        shm_a.unlink()
        shm_b.close()
        shm_b.unlink()


def measure_compute(p: int, flops_per_worker: int) -> dict:
    iters = flops_per_worker // (2 * UNROLL)
    flops_each = iters * 2 * UNROLL
    barrier = mp.Barrier(p)
    t0s, t1s = mp.Array("q", p), mp.Array("q", p)
    sums = mp.Array("d", p)
    procs = [mp.Process(target=fma_worker, args=(iters, barrier, t0s, t1s, sums, idx))
             for idx in range(p)]
    for proc in procs:
        proc.start()
    for proc in procs:
        proc.join()
    wall_ns = max(t1s) - min(t0s)
    return {"iters_per_worker": iters, "flops_per_worker": flops_each,
            "total_flops": p * flops_each, "wall_ms": wall_ns / 1e6,
            "gflops_aggregate": p * flops_each / wall_ns,
            "per_worker_gflops": [flops_each / (t1 - t0) for t0, t1 in zip(t0s, t1s)],
            "per_worker_ms": [(t1 - t0) / 1e6 for t0, t1 in zip(t0s, t1s)],
            "checksum": sum(sums)}


def roofline_ceilings(p_phys: int, buf_bytes: int, flops: int, rounds: int) -> dict:
    print(f"--- Part 3A: ceilings  P_phys={p_phys}  buffers=2x{buf_bytes / 1e6:.0f} MB  "
          f"FLOPs/worker={flops:.1e}  rounds={rounds}")
    bw_rounds = []
    for r in range(rounds):
        res = measure_bandwidth(p_phys, buf_bytes, COPY_REPS)
        bw_rounds.append(res)
        print(f"[BW round {r}] median worker copy = {res['t_median_worker_ms']:.3f} ms  "
              f"-> BW(lab formula) = {res['bw_gbs']:.2f} GB/s;  all {COPY_REPS} reps x {p_phys} workers "
              f"wall = {res['wall_ms']:.3f} ms -> BW(wall-clock aggregate) = {res['bw_wallclock_gbs']:.2f} GB/s"
              f"  verified={res['copy_verified']}")
        print("    per-worker ms: " + " ".join(f"{t:.3f}" for t in res["per_worker_ms"]))
    bw_best = sorted(bw_rounds, key=lambda r: r["bw_gbs"])[len(bw_rounds) // 2]

    cp_rounds = []
    for r in range(rounds):
        res = measure_compute(p_phys, flops)
        cp_rounds.append(res)
        print(f"[FMA round {r}] wall = {res['wall_ms']:.1f} ms  -> P = {res['gflops_aggregate']:.4f} "
              f"GFLOP/s  checksum={res['checksum']:.6f}")
        print("    per-worker GFLOP/s: " + " ".join(f"{g:.4f}" for g in res["per_worker_gflops"]))
    cp_best = sorted(cp_rounds, key=lambda r: r["gflops_aggregate"])[len(cp_rounds) // 2]

    bw, perf = bw_best["bw_gbs"], cp_best["gflops_aggregate"]
    print(f"BW_sustained = {bw:.2f} GB/s (lab formula; wall-clock aggregate of the same round = "
          f"{bw_best['bw_wallclock_gbs']:.2f} GB/s)   P_sustained = {perf:.4f} GFLOP/s   "
          f"ridge point I* = {perf / bw:.6f} FLOP/Byte")
    return {"buf_bytes": buf_bytes, "copy_reps": COPY_REPS,
            "bandwidth_rounds": bw_rounds, "compute_rounds": cp_rounds,
            "bw_sustained_gbs": bw, "bw_wallclock_gbs": bw_best["bw_wallclock_gbs"],
            "p_sustained_gflops": perf, "ridge_point": perf / bw}


# ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- Part 3B
def init_worker(name_a: str, name_b: str, name_c: str, nbytes: int) -> None:
    global _A, _B, _C
    shm_a, shm_b = attach(name_a), attach(name_b)
    _A = array("d")
    _A.frombytes(shm_a.buf[:nbytes])  # mapping may be page-rounded on macOS
    _B = array("d")
    _B.frombytes(shm_b.buf[:nbytes])
    shm_a.close()
    shm_b.close()
    _C = attach(name_c)


def store_rows(row_start: int, row_end: int, n: int, values) -> None:
    data = values if isinstance(values, array) else array("d", values)
    _C.buf[row_start * n * DOUBLE:row_end * n * DOUBLE] = data.tobytes()


def gemm_naive(task) -> None:
    """Variant 1: i-j-k, B traversed with stride N, inline index arithmetic."""
    row_start, row_end, n = task
    A, B = _A, _B
    out = []
    for i in range(row_start, row_end):
        for j in range(n):
            dot = 0.0
            for k in range(n):
                dot += A[i * n + k] * B[k * n + j]
            out.append(dot)
    store_rows(row_start, row_end, n, out)


def gemm_ikj(task) -> None:
    """Variant 2: i-k-j, a_ik hoisted, unit-stride row slices of B and C."""
    row_start, row_end, n = task
    A, B = _A, _B
    b_rows = [B[k * n:(k + 1) * n] for k in range(n)]
    out = array("d")
    for i in range(row_start, row_end):
        c_row = [0.0] * n
        base = i * n
        for k in range(n):
            a_ik = A[base + k]
            b_row = b_rows[k]
            for j in range(n):
                c_row[j] += a_ik * b_row[j]
        out.extend(c_row)
    store_rows(row_start, row_end, n, out)


def gemm_blocked(task) -> None:
    """Variant 3: 6-loop BSxBS blocking, micro-kernel order (i,k,j) with a_ik hoisted."""
    row_start, row_end, n, bs = task
    A, B = _A, _B
    c_local = [0.0] * ((row_end - row_start) * n)
    for ii in range(row_start, row_end, bs):
        i_end = min(ii + bs, row_end)
        for jj in range(0, n, bs):
            j_end = min(jj + bs, n)
            for kk in range(0, n, bs):
                k_end = min(kk + bs, n)
                for i in range(ii, i_end):
                    c_off = (i - row_start) * n
                    a_off = i * n
                    for k in range(kk, k_end):
                        a_ik = A[a_off + k]
                        b_off = k * n
                        for j in range(jj, j_end):
                            c_local[c_off + j] += a_ik * B[b_off + j]
    store_rows(row_start, row_end, n, c_local)


def make_matrix(n: int, rng: random.Random) -> array:
    return array("d", (rng.random() for _ in range(n * n)))


def read_c(shm_c, nbytes: int) -> array:
    data = array("d")
    data.frombytes(shm_c.buf[:nbytes])
    return data


def max_abs_diff(x: array, y: array) -> float:
    return max(abs(a - b) for a, b in zip(x, y))


def gflops(n: int, median_ms: float) -> float:
    return 2.0 * n ** 3 / (median_ms * 1e6)


def bench_variant(pool, fn, tasks, n, warmup, timed, label) -> dict:
    stats = run_benchmark(lambda: pool.map(fn, tasks, chunksize=1), warmup=warmup, runs=timed)
    row = {"variant": label, "min_ms": stats.min_ms, "median_ms": stats.median_ms,
           "iqr_ms": stats.iqr_ms, "gflops": gflops(n, stats.median_ms)}
    print(f"[{label:>10}] med={stats.median_ms:9.2f} ms iqr={stats.iqr_ms:7.2f} "
          f"-> {row['gflops']:.4f} GFLOP/s")
    return row


def gemm_sweep(p_phys: int, n: int, tiles, warmup: int, timed: int) -> dict:
    rng = random.Random(SEED)
    a_mat, b_mat = make_matrix(n, rng), make_matrix(n, rng)
    nbytes = n * n * DOUBLE
    shm_a = shared_memory.SharedMemory(create=True, size=nbytes)
    shm_b = shared_memory.SharedMemory(create=True, size=nbytes)
    shm_c = shared_memory.SharedMemory(create=True, size=nbytes)
    print(f"--- Part 3B: GEMM N={n}  footprint={3 * nbytes / 1e6:.2f} MB  P_phys={p_phys} "
          f"warmup={warmup} timed={timed}")
    row_ranges = partition(n, p_phys)
    tasks_plain = [(s, e, n) for s, e in row_ranges]
    rows, checks = [], {}
    try:
        shm_a.buf[:nbytes] = a_mat.tobytes()
        shm_b.buf[:nbytes] = b_mat.tobytes()
        with mp.Pool(processes=p_phys, initializer=init_worker,
                     initargs=(shm_a.name, shm_b.name, shm_c.name, nbytes)) as pool:
            pool.map(gemm_ikj, tasks_plain, chunksize=1)
            c_ref = read_c(shm_c, nbytes)
            pool.map(gemm_naive, tasks_plain, chunksize=1)
            checks["max_abs_diff_v1_vs_v2"] = max_abs_diff(read_c(shm_c, nbytes), c_ref)
            pool.map(gemm_blocked, [(s, e, n, 16) for s, e in row_ranges], chunksize=1)
            checks["max_abs_diff_v3_bs16_vs_v2"] = max_abs_diff(read_c(shm_c, nbytes), c_ref)
            checks["c_checksum"] = sum(c_ref)
            print(f"numerical check: max|C1-C2|={checks['max_abs_diff_v1_vs_v2']:.3e}  "
                  f"max|C3(BS=16)-C2|={checks['max_abs_diff_v3_bs16_vs_v2']:.3e}  "
                  f"sum(C)={checks['c_checksum']:.6f}")
            rows.append(bench_variant(pool, gemm_naive, tasks_plain, n, warmup, timed, "V1 ijk"))
            rows.append(bench_variant(pool, gemm_ikj, tasks_plain, n, warmup, timed, "V2 ikj"))
            for bs in tiles:
                tasks = [(s, e, n, bs) for s, e in row_ranges]
                row = bench_variant(pool, gemm_blocked, tasks, n, warmup, timed, f"V3 BS={bs}")
                row["bs"] = bs
                row["tile_footprint_bytes"] = 3 * bs * bs * DOUBLE
                row["intensity_flop_per_byte"] = bs / 12.0
                rows.append(row)
    finally:
        for shm in (shm_a, shm_b, shm_c):
            shm.close()
            shm.unlink()
    best = max((r for r in rows if "bs" in r), key=lambda r: r["gflops"])
    print(f"best tile BS*={best['bs']} -> {best['gflops']:.4f} GFLOP/s "
          f"(footprint {best['tile_footprint_bytes']} B);  V1={rows[0]['gflops']:.4f}  "
          f"V2={rows[1]['gflops']:.4f} GFLOP/s")
    return {"n": n, "footprint_bytes": 3 * nbytes, "warmup_runs": warmup, "timed_runs": timed,
            "row_ranges": row_ranges, "checks": checks, "rows": rows, "best_bs": best["bs"]}


def main() -> None:
    args = build_parser(__doc__).parse_args()
```

```python
"""Phase 4: skewed triangular workload under block-static, cyclic-static and shared-counter
dynamic chunk scheduling, with per-worker load-imbalance telemetry (task4_scheduling.py)."""
import math
import multiprocessing as mp
import time

from harness import run_benchmark, emit_verify_tag
from labutil import (build_parser, resolve_student_id, results_dir, save_json,
                     hardware_info, median)

N_ITEMS = 8_000
CHUNK_SIZES = [1, 8, 64, 512]
WARMUP, TIMED = 1, 5


def work_item(i: int) -> float:
    """K(i) = 1 + floor((i/40)^2) heavy sin/cos/sqrt iterations (exact integer form)."""
    k = 1 + (i * i) // 1600
    sin, cos, sqrt = math.sin, math.cos, math.sqrt
    x = 0.5 + (i % 97) * 0.01
    for _ in range(k):
        x = sqrt(1.0 + sin(x) * cos(x))
    return x


def block_worker(t, p, n, times, sums) -> None:
    t0 = time.perf_counter_ns()
    acc = 0.0
    for i in range(t * n // p, (t + 1) * n // p):
        acc += work_item(i)
    times[t] = (time.perf_counter_ns() - t0) / 1e6
    sums[t] = acc


def cyclic_worker(t, p, n, times, sums) -> None:
    t0 = time.perf_counter_ns()
    acc = 0.0
    for i in range(t, n, p):
        acc += work_item(i)
    times[t] = (time.perf_counter_ns() - t0) / 1e6
    sums[t] = acc


def dynamic_worker(t, n, chunk, counter, times, sums, claims) -> None:
    t0 = time.perf_counter_ns()
    acc = 0.0
    n_claims = 0
    lock = counter.get_lock()
    while True:
        with lock:
            start = counter.value
            counter.value = start + chunk
        if start >= n:
            break
        n_claims += 1
        for i in range(start, min(start + chunk, n)):
            acc += work_item(i)
    times[t] = (time.perf_counter_ns() - t0) / 1e6
    sums[t] = acc
    claims[t] = n_claims


def run_static(target, p, n, times, sums) -> None:
    procs = [mp.Process(target=target, args=(t, p, n, times, sums)) for t in range(p)]
    for proc in procs:
        proc.start()
    for proc in procs:
        proc.join()


def run_dynamic(p, n, chunk, counter, times, sums, claims) -> None:
    counter.value = 0
    procs = [mp.Process(target=dynamic_worker, args=(t, n, chunk, counter, times, sums, claims))
             for t in range(p)]
    for proc in procs:
        proc.start()
    for proc in procs:
        proc.join()


def imbalance(worker_ms) -> float:
    return max(worker_ms) / (sum(worker_ms) / len(worker_ms))


def bench_strategy(label, run_fn, p, times, sums, warmup, timed, extra=None) -> dict:
    snapshots = []

    def trial() -> None:
        run_fn()
        snapshots.append(list(times))

    stats = run_benchmark(trial, warmup=warmup, runs=timed)
    timed_snaps = snapshots[-timed:]
    lambdas = [imbalance(s) for s in timed_snaps]
    row = {"strategy": label, "min_ms": stats.min_ms, "median_ms": stats.median_ms,
           "iqr_ms": stats.iqr_ms,
           "lambda_median": median(lambdas), "lambda_runs": lambdas,
           "worker_ms_last": timed_snaps[-1],
           "worker_ms_max": max(timed_snaps[-1]), "worker_ms_mean": sum(timed_snaps[-1]) / p,
           "checksum": sum(sums)}
    if extra:
        row.update(extra)
    print(f"[{label:>16}] T_wall med={stats.median_ms:9.1f} ms iqr={stats.iqr_ms:7.1f}  "
          f"lambda={row['lambda_median']:.3f}  "
          f"workers(ms)=" + " ".join(f"{t:.0f}" for t in timed_snaps[-1]))
    return row


def main() -> None:
    args = build_parser(__doc__).parse_args()
    sid = resolve_student_id(args.id)
    hw = hardware_info()
    p = hw["physical_cores"]
    n = N_ITEMS // 10 if args.quick else N_ITEMS
    chunks = [1, 512] if args.quick else CHUNK_SIZES
    warmup, timed = (1, 1) if args.quick else (WARMUP, TIMED)
    total_iters = sum(1 + (i * i) // 1600 for i in range(n))

    print(f"=== PHASE 4: scheduling  N={n}  p={p}  total inner iterations={total_iters}  "
          f"K(0)={1}  K(N-1)={1 + (n - 1) ** 2 // 1600}  warmup={warmup} timed={timed} ===")
    times, sums = mp.Array("d", p), mp.Array("d", p)
    claims = mp.Array("i", p)
    counter = mp.Value("i", 0)

    rows = [bench_strategy("S1 block static", lambda: run_static(block_worker, p, n, times, sums),
                           p, times, sums, warmup, timed),
            bench_strategy("S2 cyclic static", lambda: run_static(cyclic_worker, p, n, times, sums),
                           p, times, sums, warmup, timed)]
    for chunk in chunks:
        row = bench_strategy(f"S3 dynamic C={chunk}",
                             lambda: run_dynamic(p, n, chunk, counter, times, sums, claims),
                             p, times, sums, warmup, timed, extra={"chunk": chunk})
        row["claims_per_worker_last"] = list(claims)
        rows.append(row)

    dynamic_rows = [r for r in rows if "chunk" in r]
    best = min(dynamic_rows, key=lambda r: r["median_ms"])
    print(f"\nC* = {best['chunk']}  (T_wall {best['median_ms']:.1f} ms, lambda {best['lambda_median']:.3f})")
    tag = emit_verify_tag("PHASE4", sid, rows[0]["median_ms"], best["median_ms"])
    print("\n" + tag)
    save_json(results_dir(args.quick) / "phase4.json", {
        "student_id": sid, "p": p, "n": n, "total_inner_iterations": total_iters,
        "warmup_runs": warmup, "timed_runs": timed, "rows": rows, "best_chunk": best["chunk"],
        "verify": tag, "hash_inputs": {"primary": rows[0]["median_ms"], "secondary": best["median_ms"]},
    })


if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()
```
